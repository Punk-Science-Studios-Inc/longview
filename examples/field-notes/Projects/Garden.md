# Garden

A small project for the space behind the house.

## The idea

Plant a little less. Let things grow into the gaps. Keep a clear path through the middle.

- Herbs near the kitchen.
- Native flowers by the fence.
- One quiet place to sit.

The mood comes from the [coastal walk](../Journal/Coastal%20walk.md).

[GNOME navigation guidance](https://developer.gnome.org/hig/guidelines/navigation.html) is a useful reference for simple software.
